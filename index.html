Spindle

Procedural lines in the manner of Miró, drawn as you scroll.

The walk

Each line is a pen taking small steps. Its curvature drifts at random and decays back toward straight, so the line bends in long, unhurried arcs rather than jittering.

Loops and elbows

Now and then the curvature locks high for a few dozen steps and the line ties itself in a loop. Rarer events snap the heading sideways to make an elbow.

Endings

A line finishes in a dot, a small ring, a filled shape, or an inward spiral, made by letting the curvature grow on every step until the pen winds itself to a stop.

Stars and shapes

Asterisks are a few crossing strokes. Shapes are flat colour from a five-colour palette, and where two shapes overlap, the overlap takes a third colour.

Scroll

Every line is an SVG stroke whose dash offset follows the page. The drawing edge eases toward your scroll position, so a jumpy wheel still draws smoothly. Scroll back up and the pen retreats.
